# 06 - CO2 Sourcing Models, Carbon Credits (45Q / LCFS), & Atmospheric Harvesting

## 1. Executive Summary: The $CO_2$ Sourcing Landscape

Carbon dioxide is the primary atomic constituent of microalgae biomass (52% C by weight). For a **10,000 Dry MT/day** facility, the plant requires **21,666 MT of $CO_2$ per day** (~7.9 million MT $CO_2$/year).

There are **3 distinct $CO_2$ sourcing models** available, each with vastly different economics and technological mechanics:

```

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 2. Model 1: Industrial Flue Gas Co-Location (Getting PAID to Accept $CO_2$)

### Mechanics
Co-locating the algae facility adjacent to a fossil-fuel power plant (coal or natural gas combined cycle), steel mill, or cement factory allows raw flue gas ($12–15\% \text{ CO}_2 \text{ v/v}$) to be piped directly into 5-meter deep sumps integrated within the pond loops.

### Economic Windfall: Industrial Tipping Fees & Carbon Penalties
Industrial emitters face stringent carbon compliance taxes (e.g., EU Emissions Trading System @ $€70–€90/\text{ton}$ or state-level carbon caps). 

To avoid these penalties, industrial plants enter **Offtake Agreements**, paying the algae facility a **tipping fee of $15.00 – $40.00 per metric ton** to accept and sequester their emissions.

#### Financial Revenue Calculation
$$\text{Daily Tipping Fee Revenue} = 21,666 \text{ MT CO}_2\text{/day} \times \$25.00\text{/ton} = \mathbf{\$541,650 / \text{day}}$$

This revenue translates to a **-$0.40 / gallon credit** on finished biofuel production!

## 3. Model 2: Atmospheric Harvesting (Biological Direct Air Capture - DAC)

### Can Algae Harvest $CO_2$ Directly from the Atmosphere? **YES.**

Ambient air contains approximately **420 ppm $CO_2$ ($0.042\%$ v/v)**. While mechanical Direct Air Capture (DAC) machines (e.g., chemical sorbents or cryogenic freezing) cost **$200 – $600 per ton of $CO_2$ captured** due to fan power and high regeneration heat, **open raceway ponds act as massive natural DAC systems**.

### Biological DAC Reaction Mechanism
By maintaining the pond culture medium at an alkaline **pH of 9.5 – 10.5** using sodium carbonate/bicarbonate buffer chemistry, atmospheric $CO_2$ is spontaneously absorbed at the air-water interface:

$$\text{CO}_2 \text{ (g)} + \text{OH}^- \text{ (aq)} \rightleftharpoons \text{HCO}_3^- \text{ (aq)}$$

$$\text{HCO}_3^- \text{ (aq)} + \text{OH}^- \text{ (aq)} \rightleftharpoons \text{CO}_3^{2-} \text{ (aq)} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$$

Microalgae possess the enzyme **Carbonic Anhydrase (CA)**, which rapidly converts dissolved bicarbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$) back into bio-available $CO_2$ inside the cell organelle (pyrenoid) for photosynthesis.

### Federal Carbon Credits (Section 45Q Tax Credit)
Under Section 45Q of the US Internal Revenue Code (enhanced by the Inflation Reduction Act):
- **Direct Air Capture (DAC) with Utilization**: Receives **$130.00 per metric ton of $CO_2$** captured from ambient air and converted into durable fuels or products.

#### DAC Credit Impact per Gallon
$$\text{45Q DAC Credit} = \frac{21,666 \text{ MT CO}_2 \times \$130.00/\text{ton}}{1,357,018 \text{ gal diesel}} = \mathbf{-\$2.07 / \text{gallon credit}}$$

## 4. Model 3: Biogenic $CO_2$ from Corn Ethanol Distilleries

### Mechanics
Corn ethanol fermentation yields a concentrated waste gas stream that is **$>99\%$ pure biogenic $CO_2$**:

$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 \xrightarrow{\text{Yeast}} 2 \text{ C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 2 \text{ CO}_2 \uparrow$$

### Advantages
- Requires **zero cleaning or flue-gas scrubbing** equipment.
- **Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS) Multiplier**: Fuels produced using biogenic $CO_2$ receive premium Carbon Intensity (CI) scores under California LCFS and Oregon CFP regulations, yielding additional credits of **$0.30 – $0.80 / gallon**.
