Process Engineer Narrative & Design Intent

Cultivation utilizes shallow open raceways (20-30 cm deep) covered by semitransparent agrivoltaic PV panels. Panels absorb Green & NIR light (generating 100 MW power) while transmitting Red (660nm) and Blue (430nm) PAR light to algae. Deep 5m sumps achieve 88% CO2 absorption. Vortex hydrofoils induce flashing-light kinetics, while TLA antenna strains double yield to 38-48 g/m²/day.
